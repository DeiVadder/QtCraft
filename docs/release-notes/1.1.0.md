## QtCraft 1.1.0 – the Nether

A voxel sandbox game written from scratch in C++23 with Qt 6.12. 1.1.0 opens a second dimension: the Nether, with its own generator, creatures and structures, plus brewing, netherite, a three-headed boss and the beacon. Textures and sounds are still generated by the game itself; the music now comes from 14 tracks by Scott Buckley, and the generated music stays available as an option. Packages for macOS, Windows and Linux are attached below.

### New since 1.0.0

- **Dimensions and portals:** a world now has more than one dimension. Build a frame of obsidian (4 × 5 up to 23 × 23) and light it to open a Nether portal; standing in it for 4 seconds (at once in creative mode) takes you across, and creatures, items and minecarts travel too. One block in the Nether is eight in the Overworld; a portal on the other side is found or built for you. Ruined portals in the Overworld can be lit once their frame is complete. Entering a dimension shows a loading screen; compasses and clocks spin where they have no meaning, and maps belong to the dimension they were made in.
- **The Nether:** a roofed world between bedrock floor and ceiling over a lava sea, with five biomes: Nether Wastes, Soul Sand Valley, Crimson forest, Warped forest and Basalt deltas. Glowstone hangs from the ceiling; nether quartz ore, nether gold ore and, deep down and hidden, ancient debris lie in the netherrack. Lava flows farther and faster there, water evaporates, and beds explode. Each biome has its own fog, particles (ash, spores, souls, dripping lava and the tears of crying obsidian), ambience and music.
- **New blocks:** netherrack, soul sand and soul soil, glowstone, the quartz family, nether bricks and red nether bricks, basalt, the blackstone family, soul fire, soul torch and soul lantern, nether wart (grows on soul sand), crimson and warped stems, planks and their wood families (they do not burn), nylium, fungi that grow into huge fungi with bone meal, wart blocks, shroomlight, weeping and twisting vines, roots and sprouts, and the daylight sensor.
- **Structures:** the Nether fortress with bridges, corridors, a spawner platform of Ember Wraiths and nether wart gardens; the bastion ruin in four forms (bridge, stables, treasure hall, quarter) with gold and its own loot; ruined portals in the Nether.
- **Creatures:** the Rottusk, the Magma Slime, the Wailer that spits fireballs you can hit back, the Lava Wader that walks on lava and can be ridden with a saddle and a fungus on a stick, the Ember Wraith and the Charred Skeleton of the fortress, the Tusklings that trade gold ingots for goods and turn hostile when you are not wearing gold, the Bristleboar and the Tuskling Bruiser of the bastions. The witch now throws and drinks the potion that suits the moment and appears again among the monsters of the Overworld, not only at her hut; villagers can become clerics, with the brewing stand as their workplace.
- **Brewing:** the brewing stand brews with ember dust as fuel; potions start from water bottles and nether wart, can be made longer, stronger or turned into splash potions, and some ingredients turn a potion into its opposite. The cauldron now holds three bottles of water. Tipped arrows come from eight arrows around a splash potion.
- **Effects:** speed, strength, weakness, regeneration, instant health and damage, fire resistance, water breathing, night vision, invisibility, jump boost, slow falling, haste, resistance, wither, absorption and saturation, shown in the HUD.
- **Hunger with saturation:** every food gives hunger and hidden saturation; exertion uses up saturation first. With a full bar you heal quickly.
- **Netherite:** netherite scrap from ancient debris, netherite ingots, and the smithing table, which upgrades diamond tools and armour with a netherite upgrade template and keeps their enchantments and name. Netherite items do not burn in lava. Armour now has toughness and knockback resistance, with a new damage formula.
- **The Charred Hydra:** build a T of soul sand or soul soil, put three charred skulls on top, and a three-headed boss rises. It shows a boss bar, shields itself against arrows below half health and drops a Nether Star. Whether its explosions break blocks follows the game rule `mobGriefing`.
- **The beacon:** made from a Nether Star, obsidian and glass, placed on a pyramid of up to four levels of iron, gold, emerald, diamond or netherite blocks. Its screen lets you choose a primary and, on four levels, a secondary power; tinted glass colours its beam.
- **Starting in the Nether:** a new world option "Start in" lets you begin in the Nether, on safe ground near a forest. The soul anchor (crying obsidian and glowstone, charged with glowstone) sets your respawn point there. The world map preview when creating a world shows the Overworld or the Nether, with fortresses and bastions.
- **Villages:** village chests now hold loot that suits their style and building (in newly generated villages, see below).
- **Music:** 14 tracks by Scott Buckley (CC-BY 4.0) for the menus, day, night, caves and the Nether biomes. Settings → Audio switches between "Scott Buckley tracks" and "Generated"; the generated music has been reworked after the general traits of the tracks. The audio page also notes that videos or streams with these tracks may get a YouTube Content ID claim, so switch to generated music when you record or stream. The credit lines are in the handbook chapter "Music", on the new page Settings → About and in `CREDITS.md`.
- **Volume per kind of sound:** eight sliders under the master volume: Music, Weather, Blocks, Hostile creatures, Friendly creatures, Players, Ambience and Interface. Your old effects volume carries over.
- **Smaller changes:** obsidian and crying obsidian need a diamond pickaxe; the chunk loader is a creative-only block (placed ones keep working); the handbook has new chapters for portals, the Nether, starting in the Nether, brewing, the Charred Hydra, the beacon, music and switching a world to a newer generator; the Showcase world has stations for all of it and a working portal into its own Nether. Fixed: a crash when a multiplayer connection over TLS closed after the other side had reset it.

### Worlds and compatibility

- **Overworld generator version 3** is the default for new worlds: village chests with loot and netherrack in the rubble of ruined portals. Versions 1 and 2 stay frozen and unchanged.
- **The Nether has its own generator series,** starting with Nether version 1, frozen with this release. Worlds from 1.0.0 and 0.1.0 get the Nether too: it is created the first time someone enters it.
- **Opening a world from 1.0.0 or 0.1.0:** the game asks once whether to keep the world's generator version (the default) or switch to the newer one. A switch keeps everything already explored exactly as it was; only land never generated before comes from the new generator. "Switch" leaves a hard border; "Switch with transition" blends a ring of 2 to 16 chunks (default 6) around the explored land from old to new, in the Overworld only (other dimensions get a hard border). A backup is made first, and cancelling leaves the world untouched. "Don't ask again" stops the question for that version; Settings → Advanced → "Show world generator questions again" brings it back. Dedicated servers never switch on their own: use `qtcraft-headless --convert-generator <world> --to <version> --mode hard|blend`.
- Villages that were already generated stay as they were, also after a switch: their chests stay empty, only new villages have loot.
- A world opened in 1.1.0 is saved in a newer format and no longer opens in 1.0.0; keep a backup if you want to go back.
- Water bottles no longer stack (they stacked to 16 in 1.0.0). Larger stacks in a 1.0.0 world are split when it loads; what does not fit is dropped, and with a full inventory it can be lost.
- **Protocol 97:** client and server must use the same release; 1.1.0 cannot play together with 1.0.x.

### Known limitations

- **No End yet:** there is no End dimension, so no dragon's breath and no lingering potions from the brewing stand. Lingering potions exist as items for mods and behave like splash potions with a quarter of the duration.
- **Potions:** water breathing, leaping, slow falling and the turtle master potion cannot be brewed yet, because their ingredients (pufferfish, rabbit's foot, turtle shell, phantom membrane) do not exist yet. A few trades and loot entries of the Tusklings and bastions wait for items that are still missing (soul speed, spectral arrows, crossbow and others).
- **Sound volumes:** projectile sounds (arrows, fireballs, hydra skulls) count under "Players", even when a creature fired them; shared hit and death sounds count under "Friendly creatures".
- **The Charred Hydra:** there are no difficulty levels, it fights with the values of "Normal"; its side heads turn with its body; it only rises from an upright T.
- **Starting in the Nether:** in the Overworld of such a world the compass points to Nether coordinates, and lava can flow towards the first spawn point after the world was created.
- **Riding on lava:** logging out while riding a Lava Wader far out on a lava lake puts you back above the lava when you log in.
- **Transition when switching generators:** only the Overworld gets a blended ring; the land in the ring is baked with the content and mods installed at the time of the switch.
- **Texture packs:** the HD packs and the realistic mode have no textures or material maps of their own for the new Nether blocks yet.
- **Music:** mods that are switched on or off while the game runs change the music only after the next start. Generated pieces last at most five minutes.
- **macOS:** needs macOS 14.4 or newer, because Qt 6.12 does. The app is not notarised: on macOS 15 and later try to open it once, then allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway; on macOS 14 right-click (Control-click) the app and choose Open.
- **Windows:** the program is not signed; SmartScreen asks on the first start (More info → Run anyway).
- **Linux:** the AppImage needs glibc 2.39 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13, Fedora 40 or newer) and OpenSSL 3 from the system. There is no ARM64 AppImage yet.
